# Gate 1 Policy Prerequisite Status

Status: Draft release-policy artifacts present; finalization open
Recorded: 2026-07-26

The production repository now contains draft contributor/DCO and inbound
licensing terms in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, trademark guidance in `TRADEMARKS.md`,
privacy principles in `PRIVACY.md`, and user ownership and sharing terms in
`USER-DATA-TERMS.md`. These drafts make the approved principles visible but
are not final release policies or substitutes for qualified legal review.

Before a public integrated release, FORGE must review and finalize policies
that cover:

- contributor sign-off and inbound licensing;
- use of FORGE and upstream trademarks;
- user ownership, local-first handling, retention, export, and opt-in sharing;
- hosted-service boundaries, if any; and
- how these policies are surfaced in the product and release materials.

No public integrated distribution is authorized while these drafts remain
unreviewed or incomplete.
