# FORGE Privacy Principles

Status: Draft release policy; qualified legal review required

FORGE is designed local-first. The user controls local design files, profiles,
printer configuration, evidence, logs, missions, and produced artifacts. FORGE
does not claim ownership of that material merely because the software reads,
transforms, or records it.

Remote services, shared storage, shared compute, telemetry, diagnostics, AI
processing, or hosted operation require clear, informed, revocable opt-in.
The product must explain what leaves the device, why it is requested, where it
goes, how long it is retained, and how the user can stop sharing and export or
delete shared material where technically and legally available.

FORGE must minimize collection, protect local and shared data, avoid hidden
tracking, and never use a slicer, twin, simulation, or AI system as a reason to
bypass local authority. Safety and security records may be retained when
needed for accountability, with plain-language disclosure and access controls.

This document states design principles, not a complete jurisdiction-specific
privacy notice. Final terms, retention schedules, subprocessors, and contact
channels require qualified legal review before any hosted or telemetry-
enabled release.
