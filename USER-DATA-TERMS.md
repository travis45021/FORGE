# FORGE User-Data Terms

Status: Draft release policy; qualified legal review required

1. **Ownership.** You retain ownership and control of your designs, CAD/STL/
   3MF/G-code files, printer and material data, profiles, local knowledge,
   evidence, logs, missions, and manufactured-output records, subject to
   rights held by others in material you import.
2. **Permission to operate.** You grant FORGE only the limited permission
   needed to store, inspect, validate, transform, simulate, slice, or transmit
   data when you request or authorize that action. This permission is scoped,
   visible, revocable, and cannot silently become a broader license.
3. **Sharing.** No remote sharing, telemetry, hosted processing, or AI data use
   is enabled by implication. Each such use requires informed opt-in and a
   clear explanation of recipients, purpose, retention, and withdrawal.
4. **Exports and deletion.** The product should provide practical export of
   user-owned records and deletion of shared copies where feasible, subject to
   safety, security, fraud-prevention, legal-retention, and backup limits that
   are disclosed plainly.
5. **Authority.** Uploading, printing, or starting hardware remains subject to
   FORGE's authorization model and the mandatory v1 final user confirmation.
   Data processing never grants physical authority.

These draft terms do not replace a contract or jurisdiction-specific privacy
notice. They require qualified legal review and implementation verification
before release.
