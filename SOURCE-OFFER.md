# Corresponding-Source Offer (Draft)

Status: Draft; not a valid release offer until Gate 1 is closed

For any future FORGE distribution containing Orca-derived or other copyleft
covered work, the release must provide complete corresponding source for the
exact build, including build scripts, patches, dependency notices, generated
inputs required for reproduction, and the applicable license texts.

The release record must identify:

- exact source commit and archive digest;
- binary/package digest and build environment;
- a durable source download or written-offer mechanism;
- offer duration and geographic availability;
- third-party source and notice locations; and
- security-update and source-refresh responsibility.

No URL, written offer, or release package is asserted by this draft. Qualified
legal review and reproducible-build evidence are required before publication.
