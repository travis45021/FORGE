# FORGE Trademark and Branding Guidance

Status: Draft release policy; qualified legal review required

FORGE names, logos, and marks identify the FORGE project and its maintainers.
Use the marks accurately and do not imply endorsement, certification,
partnership, sponsorship, or hardware compatibility that FORGE has not
approved.

You may truthfully describe unmodified copies, forks, integrations, and
compatibility work, but must make the relationship clear. Do not use FORGE
branding for a modified distribution in a way that suggests it is an official
release. Do not use upstream OrcaSlicer, Bambu, printer, or other third-party
marks as though they belong to FORGE; follow each owner’s published rules.

The project may require a renamed build or additional attribution where needed
to prevent confusion. This guidance does not grant rights in third-party marks
and is subject to applicable law and final legal review.
