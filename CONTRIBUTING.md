# Contributing to FORGE

Status: Draft release policy; qualified legal review required

FORGE contributions must conform to the [FORGE Constitution](CONSTITUTION.md),
which is the project's highest authority. Contributions must preserve user
control, explainability, accessibility, hardware neutrality, local-first
operation, and the safety gates documented in the repository.

## Sign-off

Each contribution must include a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)
sign-off. By adding `Signed-off-by: Full Name <email>` to each commit, the
contributor certifies that they have the right to submit the work and that it
may be contributed under the repository's applicable license terms. Automated
or assistant-generated changes require a human maintainer who reviewed and
adopted the change to provide the sign-off.

Do not include confidential material, credentials, user files, private printer
data, or third-party code without documented permission and provenance.

## Review requirements

Changes affecting authority, safety, privacy, licensing, upstream code,
printer control, or release artifacts require explicit maintainer review and a
record in the decision/evidence system. A passing test does not override the
Constitution or an open release gate.

This policy is a draft. The project will publish the final inbound-license,
DCO, and contributor terms after qualified legal review.
